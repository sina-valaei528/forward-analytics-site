Who this is for

The R&D lead or CEO with no IPR department, where "what are competitors patenting?" has no internal owner. A wrong taxonomy is the one failure that produces a confident, plausible, wrong report — and you know your industry better than any classification code does.

Method

  • Families counted at worldwide simple-family level by earliest priority year, not publication date.
  • Technology areas defined by CPC classification, filtered to the codes producers actually use.
  • Recent years corrected for publication lag.
  • Trends are Theil-Sen slopes with Mann-Kendall significance.
  • Influence is scored from forward citations counted at family level, self-citations excluded.

Limits, stated up front

This is a screening instrument, not a freedom-to-operate search. Influence scores are withheld for recent priority years that have not had time to accrue citations. Citation counts structurally understate companies that file in few jurisdictions.
